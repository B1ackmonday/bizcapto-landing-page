Good questions

Which languages can it read?

English reads well. Thai is supported too and still improving, so some words may come out wrong. That is why you review every card before saving.

Do I need the internet?

No. Scanning, reading and saving all happen on the phone. Only the LinkedIn button opens your browser, and only when you tap it.

When can I download it?

BizCapto is still in development and is not on Google Play yet. It will need Android 7.0 or newer.

Is there an iPhone version?

It is planned. Android comes first and the iPhone version is still being built.

Which languages does the app use?

The app follows your phone's language. English and Thai are available, and more languages are planned.

What happens if I change phones?

Export your cards to Excel on the old phone and import the file on the new one. There is no cloud copy, so keep that file safe.
